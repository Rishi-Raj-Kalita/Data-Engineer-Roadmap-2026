# 60-Day Roadmap for Data Engineers (2026)

A structured, interview-focused roadmap designed to help Data Engineers become **interview-ready for top product companies** using real-world scenarios, production-grade projects, and recent interview trends.

---

## Who Can Benefit from This Roadmap

* Experienced Data Engineers with **3 to 10 years of experience**
* Freshers and new joiners aiming for **Data Engineer roles** who want to practice at an advanced level

---

## Why This Roadmap Exists

* Lack of structured, interview-ready content for experienced Data Engineers
* Most SQL and PySpark questions available online do not reflect real interview difficulty
* Limited exposure to **production-ready projects**, best practices, and CI/CD workflows
* Absence of practical guidance to improve **HR screening success and interview callbacks**
